About

Hi there! My name is Stevie and my journey as a graphic designer isn’t quite what anyone would expect. I began my professional career as a Family Law Paralegal where drafting, formatting, and typesetting legal pleadings introduced me to the fascinating world of typography. From there, I became engrossed in learning and understanding—not just type—graphic design. For about two years, I enrolled in online courses from CalArts and LinkedIn Learning, I enrolled in graphic design courses at my local community college, and secured a six-month internship with a local freelance graphic designer. At some point, I was able to secure a professional design position at a local media group that produces a weekly newspaper, bimonthly magazine, and annual nonprofit books, among other projects. Since then, I have immensely strengthened my skills in graphic design, particularly in typesetting, page layout, grid systems, print production, and prepress preparation. I feel tremendously fulfilled in this industry. Moving forward, it is my passion to help keep print design alive.

Let me know what you would like to achieve and I will make it my goal to create something you will be proud to call your own.
